## Overview

YOUNITAL is an Excel-based business management and
financial reporting system designed for an aluminum
and kitchen manufacturing business.

## Business Problem

The business needed a centralized system to monitor
orders, payments, expenses, employees, materials,
and profitability.

## Objectives

• Order management
• Payment tracking
• Expense monitoring
• Labor cost analysis
• Material management
• Profitability analysis
• Management reporting
